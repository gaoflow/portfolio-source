Methods

  • Fourteen STEP parts are converted and repaired for an OpenFOAM half-car external-flow workflow.
  • A corrected one-million-cell pilot is advanced for 400 steady iterations with moving-ground and staged numerical controls.
  • A batch-means confidence-interval criterion selects the averaging window for drag and lift coefficients.
  • A zero-failure gate now covers nineteen OpenFOAM mesh outcomes plus one external volume-topology attempt, including bounded surface repair and a discrete-STL Gmsh route, before solver launch.

Limitations

  • The published coefficients are pipeline-validation values from a coarse pilot, not production aerodynamic predictions.
  • The 25–35 million-cell baseline and mesh/model sensitivity programme have not run.
  • The third-party geometry is reconstructed from public imagery and is not official team CAD.
  • All twenty bounded qualification paths failed: the best uniform-envelope topology retains eight face-interior skew failures, both tested repaired-surface meshes retain fifteen, and Gmsh generates no volume elements from the overlapping discrete boundary.
