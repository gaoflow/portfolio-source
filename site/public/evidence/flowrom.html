Methods

  • Independent FlowLab generation of 480 full-field velocity snapshots.
  • Mean-subtracted singular-value POD with orthogonal held-out projection.
  • Rank-12 exact DMD trained on eight cycles and forecast over four later cycles.

Reproduce

  • node projects/flowrom/scripts/generate-snapshots.mjs
  • cd projects/flowrom && python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
  • cd projects/flowrom && python3 scripts/analyse.py

Limitations

  • The controlled response contains one imposed periodic frequency.
  • Training and holdout share geometry, Reynolds number, and forcing amplitude.
  • The result does not establish turbulent external-aerodynamics accuracy.
