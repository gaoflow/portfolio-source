Methods

  • Laminar Darcy friction f=64/Re below Re=2300.
  • Colebrook equation solved by Newton iteration from a Haaland initial guess.
  • Minor losses via summed K coefficients; series-network flow by monotone bisection.
  • Pump/system operating point by Newton with central-difference derivative, cross-checked by brute-force scan.

Reproduce

  • cd projects/pipe-flow-sizing && python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
  • cd projects/pipe-flow-sizing && python3 scripts/analyse.py
  • cd projects/pipe-flow-sizing && python3 scripts/publish_site.py

Limitations

  • Steady, incompressible, single-phase flow only; no transients or compressibility.
  • No cavitation or NPSH modelling.
  • K coefficients are declared handbook order-of-magnitude values with wide published uncertainty.
  • The pump curve is a declared quadratic model, not vendor data.
  • Hard laminar/turbulent switch at Re=2300; series topology only.
