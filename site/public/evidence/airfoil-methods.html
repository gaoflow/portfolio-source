Methods

  • Thin-airfoil theory and Prandtl–Glauert correction at Mach 0.15.
  • Cosine-spaced Hess–Smith source/vortex panels with a Kutta condition.
  • Linear-range comparison and 40/80/160/240-panel refinement.

Reproduce

  • cd projects/airfoil-methods && python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
  • cd projects/airfoil-methods && python3 scripts/analyse.py
  • cd projects/airfoil-methods && python3 scripts/build_demo.py

Limitations

  • One symmetric airfoil, Reynolds number, Mach number, and transition condition.
  • Potential flow cannot predict boundary layers, viscous drag, separation, or stall.
  • The validation range is explicitly limited to -4.1 through 10.2 degrees.
