Methods

  • Published component curves are digitised and coupled at matched air and hydraulic operating points.
  • An 80-cell finite-volume coolant model resolves baseline, 10-second peak, and recovery phases.
  • Spatial and time-step studies quantify numerical sensitivity before the system decision is published.
  • A four-grid OpenFOAM fan-jump/porous-core surrogate qualifies the numerical coupling without claiming installed-vehicle CFD.

Reproduce

  • cd ../fsae_cooling_system && python3 -m unittest discover -s sim/zero_d -v
  • cd ../fsae_cooling_system && python3 -m unittest discover -s sim/openfoam -v
  • python3 projects/fsae-cooling/scripts/publish.py

Limitations

  • The CWA150 curve remains secondary-hosted and is conservatively biased; final approval requires first-party or measured hydraulic data.
  • Motor liquid heat fraction and KW26 operating-point loss remain uncertain and are swept rather than invented.
  • This is a coolant-domain screen, not solid-component conjugate heat transfer or a completed vehicle installation.
  • The OpenFOAM case is a shrouded proxy with no installed sidepod, wheel, leakage or measured boundary conditions.
