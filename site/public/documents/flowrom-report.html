FlowROM technical report

FlowROM: reduced-order analysis of an unsteady cavity

Question

Can a compact linear representation recover and forecast a controlled unsteady component in full-field CFD-like velocity data? The study compares two related but distinct decompositions:

  • POD asks which orthonormal spatial basis captures the most fluctuation energy.
  • DMD asks which spatial modes evolve with a single complex growth rate and frequency.

The test is intentionally narrow. FlowLab supplies auditable, independently generated fields; the known input frequency provides a ground-truth temporal target. It is a numerical-method study, not a claim about vehicle aerodynamics.

Data provenance

The snapshot generator imports the FlowLab D2Q9 BGK solver. It converges a 48×4848\times48 lid-driven cavity at Re=100Re=100 with base lid speed U=0.05U=0.05 lattice units, then varies the driving speed as

U(n)=0.05+0.01sin⁡(2πn/400).U(n)=0.05+0.01\sin\left(2\pi n/400\right).

After four settling cycles, it records uu and vv at every fluid cell every 10 solver iterations for 12 forcing cycles. This produces a matrix X∈ℝ4608×480X\in\mathbb{R}^{4608\times480}. The first 320 columns (eight cycles) train both models; the final 160 columns (four cycles) are reserved for evaluation. The imposed frequency is therefore 10/400=0.02510/400=0.025 cycles per snapshot.

POD method

The training mean x‾\bar{x} is removed and the centered matrix is factored with the singular value decomposition

X′=X−x‾=UΣVT.X'=X-\bar{x}=U\Sigma V^T.

The first rr columns of UU form the rank-rr spatial basis. New snapshots are reconstructed by orthogonal projection, X̂′=UrUrTX′\hat{X}'=U_rU_r^TX'. Energy fractions are σi2/∑jσj2\sigma_i^2/\sum_j\sigma_j^2. This follows the snapshot/POD interpretation introduced for coherent flow structures by Sirovich [1].

The first two modes retain 99.973% of training fluctuation energy. They represent the dominant quadrature pair associated with the imposed periodic response. Held-out relative fluctuation error decreases monotonically:

At rank 8, storing one mean, eight spatial modes, and eight time coefficients per snapshot uses 48.8× fewer scalar values than the original matrix.

DMD method

Exact DMD uses consecutive centered snapshot blocks X1X_1 and X2X_2. A rank-12 SVD of X1X_1 forms the reduced operator

Ã=UrTX2VrΣr−1,\tilde{A}=U_r^TX_2V_r\Sigma_r^{-1},

whose eigenpairs produce exact DMD modes Φ=X2VrΣr−1W\Phi=X_2V_r\Sigma_r^{-1}W [2,3]. Modal amplitudes come from a least-squares fit to the first training snapshot. The model then advances autonomously across all 480 sample times; it does not see the final four cycles during fitting.

The strongest oscillatory mode is 0.02500023 cycles per snapshot. Relative error against the known 0.025 forcing frequency is 9.27×10−69.27\times10^{-6} (0.00093%). Over the four held-out cycles:

  • full-state relative Frobenius error is 0.100%;
  • fluctuation-only relative error is 1.41%.

The full-state figure is smaller because the steady cavity mean contains most of the velocity norm. Both are reported to avoid hiding that denominator effect.

Interpretation and limits

This case supports three bounded conclusions:

  1. A two-dimensional POD subspace captures almost all energy in this deliberately single-frequency response.
  2. Higher-rank POD modes reduce residual spatial/nonlinear structure on unseen cycles without reversing the error trend.
  3. Exact DMD recovers the imposed temporal frequency and preserves phase well enough to forecast four cycles within the stated error gates.

It does not establish accuracy on turbulent, separated, moving-boundary, or parameter-varying vehicle flows. The D2Q9 BGK solver is low-order, the grid is coarse, the excitation is deterministic, and train and holdout segments share one operating condition. A production study would add independent operating points, noise sensitivity, rank-selection cross-validation, non-stationary transients, and cost comparisons against the parent solver.

Reproducibility and acceptance

python3 scripts/analyse.py regenerates results/analysis.json and the three figures. It exits nonzero unless all four observable gates pass:

  • held-out POD errors decrease with retained rank;
  • rank-8 held-out fluctuation error is below 2%;
  • the dominant DMD frequency is within 5% of the imposed value;
  • DMD full-state holdout error is below 2%.

Synthetic unit tests separately verify exact recovery of a rank-two sinusoid and frequency identification, preventing the case generator from being the sole oracle.
