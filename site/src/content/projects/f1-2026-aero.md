## Context & objectives

A full-car external aerodynamics study of a 2026-regulation F1 car, executed entirely in OpenFOAM. Methodology depth is benchmarked against three public studies: Penner (FW43B, STAR-CCM+), Ted L. (2026 full car, AirShaper/AMR), and Wacomber (F2004, OpenFOAM) — the target is an article-grade report with the same methodological rigour.

**Geometry credibility statement:** the geometry is a third-party reconstruction from track video/photos (pre-season testing spec), not official team CAD. All absolute force values carry this caveat; the study is trend- and methodology-oriented, as are all three references.

## Methodology

- Steady RANS, k-ω SST; half-car symmetry; 50 m/s with moving ground and rotating wheels (`rotatingWallVelocity`).
- snappyHexMesh: base 10 mm; 5 mm wings/tyres; 2.5 mm diffuser/floor edges; 3-level wake refinement; 5–6 prism layers targeting body y+ 30–150, tyres ≤210 with 0.5 mm roughness (`nutkRoughWallFunction`).
- Schemes: GAMG pressure + PBiCGStab; first-order turbulence quantities (second-order oscillates — Wacomber's finding, confirmed), momentum upgraded to linearUpwind after a 100-step first-order start.
- Convergence by force-coefficient stability (±0.5%/1000 iter), averaging from 2000; Mockett confidence-interval criterion if compute allows.
- Sensitivity programme: 15M/30M/45M mesh independence, tyre roughness 0/0.5/1.0 mm, SST vs realisable k-ε.

## Current results — pilot (pipeline validation, not physical)

The full pipeline was proven end-to-end on a coarse 1M-cell half-car mesh (6 min meshing, ~8 min solve, 4 vCPU):

| Quantity | Pilot value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Cd | 0.309 | direction & magnitude plausible for 1M coarse mesh |
| Cl | −0.351 | negative lift = downforce, as expected |
| Force stability | <1% final oscillation | steady at 400 steps |

Pilot limitations (registered for the baseline): stationary tyres (rotation destabilised rear-tyre k on the 15 mm mesh), first-order schemes, no boundary layers. Front-wing force corruption under coarse mesh + layers is the known open issue — the fix path (feature extraction, absolute leading/trailing-edge layer thickness, wing-region refinement) is designed, mirroring Penner's 0.25 mm trailing-edge sizing rationale.

## Validation

No wind-tunnel data exists for a 2026 car — the credibility framework follows Penner §8: trends credible, absolute values flagged. Mesh independence (3 levels) and turbulence-model sensitivity bound the numerics; per-part force decomposition is cross-checked against Wacomber's published F2004 contributions (floor 79% / rear wing 37% / front wing 29%).

## Failures & iterations

Maintained as a written lessons register — OpenFOAM version differences (foamRun vs simpleFoam, functionObject locations), root/mpirun pitfalls, freestream turbulence (νt/ν=10 after catching a physically wrong ω), snappyHexMesh surface-level-grade 2:1 parallel crashes (uniform (3 4) + region-driven grading), 8/14 STEP parts needing sewing, and a gmsh pathological hang solved by switching to OCP-native triangulation.

## Next steps

Baseline run on a 16 vCPU / 58 GB cloud instance: 25–35M cells with the front-wing fix, then the sensitivity programme and the article-grade report.
