## Result

The pilot pipeline works end to end; the production mesh is **NO-GO**. A corrected ≈1M-cell half-car pilot produces stable, batch-means-qualified coefficients with per-component force groups. Twenty bounded qualification paths — nineteen OpenFOAM mesh outcomes plus one external volume-topology attempt — all fail the strict mesh gate, so no qualification solver and no 25–35M production baseline has launched.

The published result is a **pipeline-validation pilot**. Three states stay separate:

- **demonstrated:** geometry preparation, meshing, steady solve, batch-means force analysis, visualisation, and component force outputs;
- **failed and diagnosed:** a coarse boundary-layer configuration that corrupted front-wing forces;
- **planned:** production mesh, mesh independence, tyre-roughness sweep, and turbulence-model sensitivity.

The geometry is a third-party reconstruction from public track imagery, not official team CAD. Even a completed production study will support method and trend claims, not confidential-team accuracy.

## Engineering question

Can an end-to-end, auditable OpenFOAM workflow be made ready for a 2026-regulation full-car study before paying for production cloud compute? The work starts with **14 STEP parts** and treats readiness as an engineering deliverable: every geometry defect, numerical failure, coefficient stream, and compute assumption must be visible before the baseline is launched.

## Pipeline

1. **Geometry.** OCP-based triangulation replaces a pathological Gmsh route, scales the model, sews repairable shells, and records unresolved gaps. Nine parts are naturally closed; difficult floor, helmet, and deflector defects remain explicit mesh inputs.
2. **Domain and mesh.** Half-car symmetry reduces compute. `blockMesh`, staged refinement, `snappyHexMesh`, feature extraction, wake regions, and prism-layer controls form one repeatable path.
3. **Boundary conditions.** The target setup uses 50 m/s inlet flow, moving ground, rotating wheel walls, and k-ω SST. Freestream turbulence was corrected after tracing an unphysical eddy-viscosity ratio.
4. **Numerics.** The pilot starts with first-order transport and upgrades momentum to `linearUpwind`; pressure uses GAMG and the remaining fields use PBiCGStab.
5. **Outputs.** `forceCoeffs` writes the whole-car history plus front wing, rear wing, floor, front tyre, rear tyre, and body groups. ParaView views cover surface pressure, wake slices, Q-criterion, mesh detail, and convergence.

## Corrected pilot result

The corrected ≈1M-cell pilot contains 301 coefficient samples from iteration 100 through 400. A batch-means confidence-interval search selects iteration **370** as the steady-window start:

| Coefficient | Steady mean | 95% CI | Relative CI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cd | 0.2581 | ±0.0017 | 0.64% |
| Cl | −0.3019 | ±0.0018 | 0.59% |

These values prove stable data plumbing and sign conventions only. They are not promoted as full-car aerodynamic predictions: the pilot is too coarse, its reference area is provisional, and the production sensitivity programme is incomplete.

## Why the window starts at 370

The pilot does not average the last arbitrary fraction of iterations. A batch-means search evaluates candidate steady windows and selects the earliest interval whose relative 95% confidence bounds meet the declared criterion. For the available history, that point is iteration 370.

The resulting 0.64% drag and 0.59% lift relative intervals quantify sampling stability inside the pilot. They exclude mesh, turbulence-model, geometry, and reference-area uncertainty, which is why the coefficients remain pipeline-validation values.

## The failed run changed the baseline

A second coarse trial added rotating wheels and boundary layers. It stayed numerically alive for 300 iterations, but the front-wing coefficient grew to approximately ±10⁴ while the other component groups remained bounded. The complete failed history is retained; its maximum absolute whole-car Cl exceeds 10,000.

That failure rejects the mesh, not the physics. A strict qualification campaign now covers twenty bounded paths: nineteen completed OpenFOAM mesh outcomes plus one external volume-topology attempt.

Conservative snap controls reduced the highly skew count from 97 to 36. Level-6 seam refinement removed every front-wing and tyre residual and reduced the total to nine. A bounded 5.208M-cell uniform envelope then improved maximum aspect ratio to 26.306, maximum non-orthogonality to 64.999°, and maximum skewness to 8.785, but retained eight face-interior failures.

The repair campaign made the aerodynamic-change boundary explicit: six front-wing surfaces were evaluated under a 0.1 mm deviation contract, with five preserving triangle connectivity and staying in budget. Both the near-zero 0.0000017 mm candidate and the 0.08368 mm budget-edge candidate produced 5.322M-cell meshes with 15 highly skew faces and one failed check. Shape smoothing did not repair the volume topology.

Two installed Gmsh paths were also exhausted. OpenCASCADE Boolean subtraction failed on the intersecting 54-solid assembly; the discrete-STL alternative tetrahedrized 3.69M nodes but rejected overlapping facets on surface 3 and wrote zero volume elements. No candidate was allowed to start the 300-step solve.

## Readiness is layered

The campaign does not reduce readiness to "the solver ran." Four layers must agree:

1. **source geometry:** the tested STEP/STL inputs must match the frozen hashes or pass an explicit shape-deviation contract;
2. **volume mesh:** `checkMesh` must complete and report zero failed checks, zero highly skew faces under the strict campaign contract, and a bounded coarse cell count;
3. **field solution:** all required fields and force outputs must remain finite through the qualification interval;
4. **aerodynamic evidence:** coefficients require a declared averaging window, component balance, grid sensitivity, and model sensitivity before promotion.

The corrected pilot demonstrates parts of layers three and four for data plumbing. The qualification campaign remains blocked at layer two.

## Failure taxonomy

| Failure class | Observed evidence | Engineering response |
|---|---|---|
| Force corruption | front-wing $|C_l|$ grows to approximately $10^4$ | reject the mesh; preserve the full history |
| Refinement discontinuity | three `hexRef8` parallel crashes | prohibit abrupt surface-level shortcuts |
| Local topology | residual skew clusters move between parts | compare variants by the same strict gate |
| Shape repair | 0.0000017 and 0.08368 mm candidates both retain 15 skew faces | reject smoothing as the repair |
| External meshing | OCC subtraction fails; discrete Gmsh writes zero volume elements | require different source topology or backend |

This classification prevents tuning solver relaxation against a geometry or mesh defect. A numerically quieter coefficient history would not make a failed volume mesh acceptable.

## Campaign controls

Every controlled mesh variant changes a named dictionary input while retaining the common source case and gate. Source-geometry hashes prevent accidental CAD drift. The separate surface-repair contract permits at most 0.1 mm deviation and requires topology-preserving triangle connectivity.

The two repaired surfaces selected for meshing span the useful contract: a near-zero conservative candidate and the passing candidate nearest the deviation budget. Their identical failed outcome shows that more smoothing within the permitted range is not a supported next action.

## Compute decision

The campaign spends cheap evidence to protect expensive compute. A 300-step qualification solve is allowed only after mesh acceptance; a 25–35M production baseline additionally requires explicit paid-compute approval. Because no mesh passes, both decisions remain false.

This NO-GO is the project result. It records what was tried, what improved, what regressed, and which prerequisite must change. Launching a production case despite the gate would add cost without making the aerodynamic claim more defensible.

## Planned production study

The production sequence is deliberately gated:

1. replace or engineer-direct the simplification of the overlapping source topology, or install a different conformal or hex-dominant meshing backend;
2. require zero failed `checkMesh` checks on the coarse qualification mesh;
3. run the gated 300-step first-order → `linearUpwind` qualification only after the mesh passes;
4. generate the 25–35M baseline only after explicit paid-compute approval;
5. apply the confidence-interval criterion, then run grid, tyre-roughness, and turbulence-model sensitivity;
6. report component balance, surface pressure, wake structures, y+, mesh quality, and uncertainty together.

No wind-tunnel dataset exists for this reconstructed 2026 car. Credibility therefore rests on numerical independence, failure disclosure, reference-method comparison, and bounded conclusions.
