## Scope boundary

This is the latest and most system-complex study in the portfolio, but its published result is still a **pipeline-validation pilot**. The 25–35 million-cell baseline has not run. The page therefore separates three states:

- **demonstrated:** geometry preparation, meshing, steady solve, batch-means force analysis, visualisation, and component force outputs;
- **failed and diagnosed:** a coarse boundary-layer configuration that corrupted front-wing forces;
- **planned:** production mesh, mesh independence, tyre-roughness sweep, and turbulence-model sensitivity.

The geometry is a third-party reconstruction from public track imagery, not official team CAD. Even a completed production study will support method and trend claims—not confidential-team accuracy.

## Engineering question

Can an end-to-end, auditable OpenFOAM workflow be made ready for a 2026-regulation full-car study before paying for production cloud compute?

The work starts with **14 STEP parts** and treats readiness as an engineering deliverable: every geometry defect, numerical failure, coefficient stream, and compute assumption must be visible before the baseline is launched.

## Pipeline

1. **Geometry.** OCP-based triangulation replaces a pathological Gmsh route, scales the model, sews repairable shells, and records unresolved gaps. Nine parts are naturally closed; difficult floor, helmet, and deflector defects remain explicit mesh inputs.
2. **Domain and mesh.** Half-car symmetry reduces compute. `blockMesh`, staged refinement, `snappyHexMesh`, feature extraction, wake regions, and prism-layer controls form one repeatable path.
3. **Boundary conditions.** The target setup uses 50 m/s inlet flow, moving ground, rotating wheel walls, and k-ω SST. Freestream turbulence was corrected after tracing an unphysical eddy-viscosity ratio.
4. **Numerics.** The pilot starts with first-order transport and upgrades momentum to `linearUpwind`; pressure uses GAMG and the remaining fields use PBiCGStab.
5. **Outputs.** `forceCoeffs` writes the whole-car history plus front wing, rear wing, floor, front tyre, rear tyre, and body groups. ParaView views cover surface pressure, wake slices, Q-criterion, mesh detail, and convergence.

## Corrected pilot result

The corrected ≈1M-cell pilot contains 301 coefficient samples from iteration 100 through 400. A batch-means confidence-interval search selects iteration **370** as the steady-window start:

| Coefficient | Steady mean | 95% CI | Relative CI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cd | 0.2581 | ±0.0017 | 0.64% |
| Cl | −0.3019 | ±0.0018 | 0.59% |

These values prove stable data plumbing and sign conventions only. They are not promoted as full-car aerodynamic predictions: the pilot is too coarse, its reference area is provisional, and the production sensitivity programme is incomplete.

## The failed run changed the baseline

A second coarse trial added rotating wheels and boundary layers. It remained numerically alive for 300 iterations, but the front-wing coefficient grew to approximately ±10⁴ while the other component groups remained bounded. The complete failed history is retained; its maximum absolute whole-car Cl exceeds 10,000.

That failure rejects the mesh—not the physics. A strict qualification campaign now covers twenty bounded paths: nineteen completed OpenFOAM mesh outcomes plus one external volume-topology attempt.

Conservative snap controls reduced the highly skew count from 97 to 36. Level-6 seam refinement removed every front-wing and tyre residual and reduced the total to nine. A bounded 5.208M-cell uniform envelope then improved maximum aspect ratio to 26.306, maximum non-orthogonality to 64.999°, and maximum skewness to 8.785, but retained eight face-interior failures.

The final repair campaign made the aerodynamic-change boundary explicit: six front-wing surfaces were evaluated under a 0.1 mm deviation contract, with five preserving triangle connectivity and remaining in budget. Both the near-zero 0.0000017 mm candidate and the 0.08368 mm budget-edge candidate produced 5.322M-cell meshes with 15 highly skew faces and one failed check. Shape smoothing did not repair the volume topology.

Two installed Gmsh paths were also exhausted. OpenCASCADE Boolean subtraction failed on the intersecting 54-solid assembly; the discrete-STL alternative tetrahedrized 3.69M nodes but rejected overlapping facets on surface 3 and generated zero volume elements. No candidate was allowed to start the 300-step solve.

Three earlier `hexRef8` parallel crashes also established a hard meshing rule: surface-level discontinuities cannot be used as a shortcut for local refinement.

## Planned production study

The production sequence is deliberately gated:

1. replace or engineer-direct the simplification of the overlapping source topology, or install a different conformal/hex-dominant meshing backend;
2. require zero failed `checkMesh` checks on the coarse qualification mesh;
3. run the gated 300-step first-order → `linearUpwind` qualification only after the mesh passes;
4. generate the 25–35M baseline only after explicit paid-compute approval;
5. apply the confidence-interval criterion, then run grid, tyre-roughness, and turbulence-model sensitivity;
6. report component balance, surface pressure, wake structures, y+, mesh quality, and uncertainty together.

No wind-tunnel dataset exists for this reconstructed 2026 car. Credibility therefore comes from numerical independence, failure disclosure, reference-method comparison, and bounded conclusions—not from matching an unavailable answer.
